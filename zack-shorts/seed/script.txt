If you swallowed a watermelon seed, you've probably been told a watermelon would start growing inside your stomach, because that's what everyone's parents say. But the moment it lands, it drops into a pool of acid with no soil and no time, so it never even gets the chance to sprout. Its tough outer shell, which your stomach can't break down, gets squeezed into your intestines, slides along with everything else you ate, and just a few days later, leaves your body exactly the way it went in. And the funny part is, if you'd actually chewed it, it would've been one of the healthiest things on your plate.

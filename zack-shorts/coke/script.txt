If you dropped a tooth in a glass of Coke, you've probably been told it would dissolve overnight, because that's what everyone's parents say. But Coke's acid is usually weaker than the acid already inside your stomach, so it can't melt a tooth in a night. Over days, it slowly softens the surface and strips away its shine. And your stomach survives far stronger acid because a thick layer of mucus coats its walls and is constantly rebuilt. The real damage comes from sipping it all day, because your teeth never get a break… so drink it quickly, then rinse with water.

If everyone on Earth peed into the Grand Canyon at the same time, you'd probably think it would fill up in a single day. But the Grand Canyon can hold over four quadrillion litres, while the average person only pees about one and a half litres a day. So even if all eight billion of us peed into it nonstop, every single day, we'd only add about twelve billion litres. At that rate, it would take over nine hundred years to fill it to the top… which means we'd have needed to start around the year eleven hundred, and that's if not a single drop evaporated.
